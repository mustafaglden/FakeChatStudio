Common questions

Where do my exports go?

Tap Save to Photos after an export and the image or video is added to your camera roll. Share hands the same file to any other app. The app keeps a temporary copy only long enough for the share sheet to finish with it, then deletes it.

Why does every export have a label on it?

Every image and video the app exports carries a small label, “Fiction · Chat Story Studio”. It says what the file is: a story somebody wrote, not a record of a conversation that took place. It is on every export, free or Pro, and it cannot be switched off or bought off.

How many videos can I make?

The free tier includes one video a day, so you can see exactly what you would be buying. Pro removes the daily limit and unlocks every chat style, group chats and 4K at 60fps.

I paid on another phone and my purchase is missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases. You do not have to hit a limit first — the restore button is always there. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

Will my chats move to a new phone?

Not on their own — not in this version. Your conversations live on the device you made them on. They are included in an iCloud device backup if you have one, so restoring a new phone from that backup brings them across. Sync between two phones at once is coming in a later update.

Is this app affiliated with any messaging service?

No. Chat Story Studio cannot send a message and cannot connect to any messaging service — there is no such code in it. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any of them, and every chat style in it is our own design.

A note on what this app is for. It is a tool for writing fiction: texting stories, scripts and dialogue. Do not use it to impersonate a real person, to fabricate evidence, or to deceive anyone. The label on every export is there so that nobody mistakes a story for a record.
