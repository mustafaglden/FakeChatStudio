Common questions

Where do my exports go?

Tap Save to Photos after an export and the image or video is added to your camera roll. Share hands the same file to any other app. The app keeps a temporary copy only long enough for the share sheet to finish with it, then deletes it.

Why is there a watermark?

Free exports carry a small mark. Pro removes it from every image and video. There is also a one-off purchase that removes it from a single file, if a subscription is not what you want.

How many videos can I make?

The free tier includes one video a day, watermarked, so you can see exactly what you would be buying. Pro removes the daily limit and the watermark, and unlocks 4K at 60fps.

I paid on another phone and my purchase is missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases. You do not have to hit a limit first — the restore button is always there. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

Will my chats move to a new phone?

Not on their own — not in this version. Your conversations live on the device you made them on. They are included in an iCloud device backup if you have one, so restoring a new phone from that backup brings them across. Sync between two phones at once is coming in a later update.

Is this app affiliated with any messaging service?

No. FakeChat Studio imitates visual styles only, and every style in it is an original design. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any messaging service. It is for entertainment and creative use.

A note on what this app is for. It makes conversations that are obviously meant as fiction, jokes, and creative work. Please do not use it to impersonate a real person, to fabricate evidence, or to deceive anyone in a way that could cause them harm.
