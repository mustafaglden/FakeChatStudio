Privacy Policy

FakeChat Studio has no account system and no backend. Your conversations stay on your devices.

Last updated 13 September 2026

SupportPrivacy

The short version

We never receive the conversations you write. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server of ours for them to be sent to.

The app collects anonymous usage statistics — which features are used, not what you type — and you can turn them off.

What stays on your device

Everything you create. The conversations, the names you give people, the messages, the photos you add, and the images and videos you export are all stored locally on your iPhone.

There is no sync in this version. Your conversations exist on the one device you made them on, and nowhere else. If your phone is backed up to iCloud they are part of that backup, which belongs to your Apple Account — we cannot see it, and it does not pass through any system of ours.

What is collected

The app reports anonymous usage events so we can tell which features matter and where people get stuck. Each event is a name and a few labels. In full, that is:

  • which chat style was chosen, and which was used for a conversation or an export
  • that a message was added — never the message
  • export format, how many messages were in it, and how long the render took
  • which screen a paywall was opened from, and which limit was reached
  • which product a purchase started or completed for
  • that onboarding finished

No message text, no names, no photos, and no exported files are ever included in any of this. There is no account to attach it to, and no advertising identifier is used or collected.

These events are processed by Google Firebase Analytics, which assigns the installation a random identifier of its own so it can tell one device's events apart from another's. That identifier is not linked to you, it is not shared with anyone, and it is gone when you delete the app.

You can turn these events off entirely in the app's Settings. Turning them off is real: with the switch off the analytics service is never started at all, so nothing is sent and nothing is held back to send later. Turning it off after the fact stops collection from that moment. Nothing else about the app changes when you do.

Photos

The app asks for permission to add to your photo library, and only when you tap Save to Photos. It does not ask for permission to read your library, and it cannot browse your photos. If you attach an image to a conversation, you pick it yourself and it stays on your device with the rest of that conversation.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app is told only whether a purchase is active.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change will be described here rather than made silently.
